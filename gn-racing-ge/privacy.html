Privacy Policy for GN Racing GE

Last updated: October 12, 2026

GN Racing GE ("the game") is developed by GN Games (GNGames2020, Georgia). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. Contact: gngames2020@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Game progress and cloud save

The game saves your progress: coins, cars, upgrades, settings, level, your in-game driver name and country flag, your paint designs and your best-lap replays. If you sign in with Google Play Games, it is saved with Google Play Games Saved Games. It is also backed up with Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) under an anonymous player ID, so you can restore it on a new device.

Online ghosts, leaderboards and achievements

When you set a lap time or drift score, the game uploads a replay of your run (which car you drove and its positions on the track) and your score. Other players can see this data and race against your replay. Scores and achievements are posted to Google Play Games, where they appear under your Google Play Games name.

Live multiplayer and free roam

When you join an online race, a free roam room or watch as a spectator, the game sends your driver name, flag, car and its look, your car's position and speed on the track, your ping and your race results to the other players in the room. This goes through Photon (Exit Games), which processes your IP address to connect you to the nearest server. Room data is not kept after the room closes.

Chat, voice and friends

If you turn on chat, your text messages and voice are delivered live to the players in the channel through Vivox (Unity Technologies). We do not record voice and do not store messages, except a message that a player reports. Friend lists, friend requests and online status are stored with Unity Friends. Chat and voice are off until you turn them on, and are not available to players under 13. Voice uses the microphone only after you allow it and only while you use voice chat.

Reports

If you report a player or a message, we receive your player ID, the reported player's ID, the reported message text, the room and the time. We use this only for moderation.

Identifiers

The game uses your Google Play Games player ID and an anonymous Unity player ID to link your save, replays, friends and online play to you.

Diagnostics

The Google Play Games, Unity and Photon SDKs may collect technical and diagnostic information to run their services.

We do not collect your e-mail address, phone number, contacts or precise location. Your age bracket for chat is kept only on your phone.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads through Unity Ads / Unity LevelPlay. These services may collect device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, general location derived from the IP address and ad interaction data for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and service operation, under their own privacy policies.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play Games Services (Google): sign-in, saved games, leaderboards, achievements. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Gaming Services, Vivox and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): cloud backup, online replays, friends, chat and voice, advertising. unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Photon (Exit Games): live multiplayer rooms. photonengine.com/privacy

We do not sell your data.

4. Deleting your data

  1. In the game: Settings > ACCOUNT > DELETE CLOUD DATA deletes your cloud backup account with your saved progress and shared online replays, and your Google Play Games saved game.
  2. By e-mail: write to gngames2020@gmail.com and include the player ID shown in Settings > ACCOUNT. We delete your cloud save, online replays, friends data and reports about you within 30 days.

Leaderboard scores and achievements are held by Google Play Games and can be managed in your Google Account and Play Games settings. Progress stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game. More: Delete your data.

5. Data retention

Cloud saves, replays and friends data are kept while your account exists and are deleted on request. Reports are kept up to 12 months. Live room data is not kept after the room closes.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Chat and voice are disabled for players who say they are under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored by Google, Unity and Photon on their secure infrastructure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
