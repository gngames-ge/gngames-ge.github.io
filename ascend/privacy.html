Privacy Policy: Ascend

Last updated: September 26, 2026

The short version

Ascend does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no servers.

What the app stores

Ascend saves two things, and both stay on your device:

  • Your best time for each course.
  • A graphics quality setting the game chooses by measuring its own frame rate, plus whether sound and ambience are on.

These are written to Android's app-preferences storage. They are not transmitted anywhere, are not readable by other apps, and are deleted when you uninstall Ascend.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or any account.
  • It shows no ads and has no analytics or crash reporting.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • It has no in-app purchases.

Permissions

Ascend requests no permissions and has no networking code: it sends and receives nothing and works the same in airplane mode.

Children

Ascend has no ads, purchases, chat or user-generated content and collects no data, so it is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the date above will change.
